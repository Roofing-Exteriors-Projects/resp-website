About REP

A contractor should make the project feel simpler, not harder.

Roofing & Exteriors Projects Ltd. is a Calgary-based exterior contractor serving homeowners, commercial properties and restoration projects. Our approach is straightforward: understand the problem, explain the options clearly, organize the work properly and leave the property clean when the job is complete.

From planned replacements to storm damage, we focus on practical recommendations, dependable communication and exterior systems designed to perform over the long term.

Storm & insurance claims

When hail hits, you need a clear next step.

Storm damage can affect roofing, siding, gutters, fascia and other exterior components at the same time. We help document visible damage, organize the repair scope and coordinate restoration work once the claim moves forward.

01

InspectWe review accessible exterior areas and document visible damage.

02

03
